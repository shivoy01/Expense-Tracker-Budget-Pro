Privacy Policy

Application: Expense Tracker & Budget Pro  |  Effective Date: October 30, 2026

Privacy Summary: Expense Tracker & Budget Pro does not collect, store, or share any of your personal or financial data. No login or registration is required, no sensitive device permissions are requested, and no third-party tracking SDKs are used. An initial internet connection is only used once to load UI icons and visual interface assets.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Expense Tracker & Budget Pro mobile application, developed and maintained by Chhtrapati Bariha ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and have designed this application to keep all your financial records strictly private on your device.

1. Zero Data Collection & No Registration

We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details (such as your name, email address, phone number, or bank details) to use Expense Tracker & Budget Pro. We do not collect or store any personal information on external servers.

2. 100% Local On-Device Data Storage

All expense entries, income records, budget limits, categories, and notes that you add in the application are stored strictly locally in your device's internal storage:

  • Complete Privacy: Neither the Developer nor any third party can view, access, or read your financial records.
  • No Cloud Transmission: Your expense data never leaves your phone and is never uploaded to any external server.

3. Limited Network Usage & Permissions

Expense Tracker & Budget Pro is built to respect your device security and privacy:

  • One-Time Network Access (Internet Permission): The application requires basic internet connectivity on initial launch solely to fetch and render application icons, fonts, and visual user interface (UI) elements. Once cached, the app functions offline, and no user or financial data is ever transmitted during this network request.
  • No Sensitive Device Permissions: The application does not request or require access to any sensitive permissions such as your camera, microphone, contacts, location, call logs, or personal media files.

4. No Third-Party Tracking or Ad SDKs

The application does not integrate any third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, or behavioral tracking SDKs. We do not track your usage behavior, collect advertising identifiers, or sell any user information to third parties.

5. Data Control & Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full ownership and control over your records at all times:

  • You can edit or delete any individual transaction, category, or budget directly inside the application.
  • Clearing the application's storage from your Android device settings or uninstalling Expense Tracker & Budget Pro will immediately and permanently delete all app data from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for general audiences and does not collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we add new features in future updates that change how data is handled, we will update this Privacy Policy and revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. You are encouraged to review this page periodically.
